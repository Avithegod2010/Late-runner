PLAY IN 3 TAPSThis is all you need. Really.

1

Tap the green PLAY button (bottom-right).You start riding immediately. No setup needed.

2

Move and jump.Phone: swipe left/right to move, tap to jump.
Keyboard: A D to move, Space to jump.

3

Crash? Get coins and tips anyway.Read the tip, then tap PLAY again. That's the whole game loop.

That's it — you know how to play.

GOOD TO KNOW5 things that make the game better

  • Red badges = free stuff. Always tap them before you ride.
  • Combos only count when you BANK them (B). An unbanked combo is lost when you crash.
  • Your game saves itself on this device and works offline. Nothing to do.
  • Combo rule: near-misses and tricks build your combo. Bank it before a hard part!
  • Play daily to grow your streak — it multiplies your score.
